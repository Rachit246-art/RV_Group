Engineered Additives & Chemistry

Specialty chemical formulation packages that empower lubricant blenders and refinery operators to achieve superior oxidative resistance.

MOLECULAR ENHANCEMENT

Formulations Designed for Harsh Operating Environments

RV Global Petroleum supplies turnkey detergent-inhibitor (DI) packages, zinc dialkyldithiophosphate (ZDDP) anti-wear agents, high molecular weight OCP polymers, and pour-point depressants tailored to mineral and synthetic base stocks.

Turnkey Blending Compatibility

Direct drop-in formulation ratios with standard Group I/II/III base oils, reducing blender trial-and-error costs and ensuring immediate API SP/CK-4 pass rates.

Detergent & Dispersant Packages

Overbased calcium sulfonates and polyisobutylene succinimides that suspend sludge and neutralize acidic fuel combustion bypass.

Viscosity Modifiers (VII)

Olefin copolymer (OCP) liquid and solid bale polymers with superior shear stability index (SSI 22 & 35) for multi-grade engine and gear lubricants.

Anti-Wear & Extreme Pressure (EP)

Sulfurized isobutylene, active phosphate esters, and primary/secondary ZDDP combinations providing unmatched boundary film defense.
