Consulting & Engineering

End-to-end petroleum engineering, refinery debottlenecking, process chemistry optimization, and custom technical consultancy for sovereign and private energy operators.

WORLD-CLASS ADVISORY

Precision Engineering for Complex Energy Challenges

RV Global Petroleum combines decades of downstream refinery operational knowledge with world-class chemical and mechanical engineering capabilities. Our engineering and consulting division collaborates with refiners, chemical processors, terminal operators, and fleet logistics enterprises to maximize yield, optimize energy efficiency, and resolve critical technical bottlenecks.

Whether formulating tailor-made synthetic lubricant blends, conducting thermal cracking feasibility assessments, or integrating automated digital metering across terminal pipelines, our licensed engineers deliver measurable economic impact and strict compliance with API, ASTM, and ISO international standards.

OUR SPECIALIZED DISCIPLINES

Comprehensive Engineering Domains

Delivering technical rigor, simulation fidelity, and tangible cost efficiencies across every stage of petroleum downstream operations.

Refinery Process Optimization

Mathematical modeling and heat integration studies designed to minimize thermal losses, increase conversion rates, and extend catalytic bed lifespans in hydroprocessing units.

  • Catalytic cracking optimization
  • Fractionation column efficiency
  • Energy recovery network redesign

Lube & Fuel Formulation R&D

Custom formulation engineering of synthetic engine lubricants, biodegradable esters, marine bunker fuels (VLSFO), and high-performance additive treat rates according to API SP, CK-4, and ACEA benchmarks.

  • Base oil interchangeability (BOI) studies
  • Viscosity modifier shear stability testing
  • Low-SAPS & Euro VI compliance testing

Terminal & Storage Engineering

Turnkey consulting for bulk liquid storage terminals, manifold design, automated custody transfer metering (LACT), marine vapor recovery systems, and hazardous area classification.

  • API 650/653 storage tank inspections
  • SCADA & automated blending systems
  • Bunkering jetty hydraulics

Decarbonization & ESG Engineering

Engineering strategies to lower carbon intensity, integrate renewable hydrotreated vegetable oil (HVO) blending, and evaluate carbon capture feasibility in traditional refinery settings.

  • Biofuel co-processing assessments
  • Flare reduction & emissions scrubbing
  • Scope 1 & Scope 2 mitigation roadmap

Failure Analysis & Tribology

Comprehensive root-cause failure analysis for rotating machinery, turbochargers, industrial gearboxes, and hydraulic systems impacted by lubrication degradation or contamination.

  • Ferrography & particulate analysis
  • FTIR chemical oxidation profiling
  • Mechanical bearing wear investigation

Regulatory & API Licensing

Guiding regional manufacturers through API Engine Oil Licensing and Certification System (EOLCS), OEM approval dossiers (MB, VW, Volvo, Cummins), and REACH/GHS classification dossiers.

  • API EOLCS filing & testing matrices
  • OEM field test coordination
  • ISO 17025 laboratory accreditation setup
