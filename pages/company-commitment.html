RESPONSIBLE ENERGY LEADERSHIP

Our Commitment to the World

Championing ethical energy trading, stringent QHSE protocols, carbon offset investments, and zero-defect downstream formulation.

1. Environmental Stewardship & ESG

We actively decrease greenhouse gas footprints by supplying low-friction Group III hydrocracked base oils that improve vehicle fuel economy by up to 3.8%. Our refining agreements emphasize closed-loop thermal recapture and low-emission gas flare mitigation.

2. API & ASTM Technical Integrity

No molecule leaves our custody without certified independent lab verification by SGS or Bureau Veritas. We maintain strict conformity to SAE viscosity grades, ASTM flash-point minimums, and ISO 4406 fluid cleanliness standards.

3. Occupational Health & Terminal Safety (QHSE)

Target Zero: Zero spills, zero injuries, zero compliance deviations. Our shore tanks, bunkering barges, and chemical transport vehicles operate under stringent HAZMAT and SOLAS maritime security frameworks.

4. Transparent Global Commercial Ethics

RV Global Petroleum adheres strictly to international sanctions, anti-corruption conventions, and fair-trade principles. Our counterparty onboarding is backed by automated KYC/AML verification protocols.
