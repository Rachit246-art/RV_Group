Mobility Services

INTELLIGENT TRANSPORT SOLUTIONS

Powering the Future of Commercial Fleet Mobility

Modern transport networks require more than conventional fuel delivery; they demand synchronized energy logistics, real-time consumption accountability, and transition-ready fluids. RV Global Petroleum’s Mobility Services division delivers turnkey fuel and lubrication infrastructure for logistics operators, municipal transit systems, port authorities, and heavy industrial fleets.

With dedicated bowser tanker fleets, automated smart-dispense systems, high-efficiency diesel exhaust fluid (DEF/AdBlue) distribution, and heavy-duty API CK-4 transport lubricants, we ensure zero fleet downtime while actively lowering operational cost-per-kilometer.

INTEGRATED SERVICES

End-to-End Fleet Mobility Offerings

Scalable infrastructure designed to optimize operational uptime, ensure fuel compliance, and reduce total cost of ownership (TCO).

On-Site Direct Fleet Refueling

Eliminate driver downtime and off-route station visits. Our calibrated mobile bowsers refuel your logistics fleet directly at your warehouse yards, distribution centers, or construction jobsites.

  • Scheduled overnight yard fueling
  • Calibrated, certified weights & measures meters
  • Zero driver overtime expenditure

Smart Fuel Cards & Analytics

Real-time transaction control with RFID vehicle tags, driver pin verification, and centralized accounting. Track fuel economy, consumption anomalies, and tax-rebate reports seamlessly.

  • Multi-network terminal acceptance
  • Odometer & GPS verification integration
  • Instant fraud alert triggers

Heavy-Duty Transport Lubrication

Comprehensive lubrication packages for commercial logistics fleets, haulage trucks, and buses. Engineered with virgin base oils to resist shear degradation and maximize heavy engine longevity.

  • API CK-4 / CJ-4 synthetic heavy-duty diesel oils
  • High-pressure commercial gear & axle fluids (GL-5)
  • Extended drain intervals exceeding 100,000 km

Bulk Depot Tank Storage Solutions

Turnkey installation of double-walled self-bunded fuel storage tanks, DEF dispensing modules, and digital automatic tank gauges (ATG) connected to our continuous replenishment cloud.

  • Containerized self-bunded tanks (10kL to 110kL)
  • Vendor-managed inventory (VMI) auto-refill
  • Environmental spill containment compliance

HVO & Sustainable Biofuels

Drop-in Hydrotreated Vegetable Oil (HVO100) and biodiesel blends meeting EN 15940 specifications, offering up to 90% net lifecycle greenhouse gas reduction without requiring engine modifications.

  • 100% fossil-free renewable diesel
  • Excellent cold-start performance down to -32°C
  • Verified ISCC-EU sustainability certification

Lubricant Health Diagnostics

Laboratory used-oil sampling service for commercial fleets. Detect engine wear metals, coolant ingress, and soot accumulation early to safely extend oil drain intervals up to 100,000 km.

  • ICP elemental spectroscopy reports within 24h
  • TBN / TAN reserve alkalinity tracking
  • Predictive maintenance alerts to avert breakdowns
